# Key Findings

1. Strict-band coordination yields modest savings (1-4%), near the physical optimum for thermally saturated buildings.
2. Comfort-band extension is the primary lever, roughly tripling villa savings to ~13%.
3. At building scale, bill reduction can exceed energy reduction because coordination lowers consumption billed at the higher Tier-2 rate.
4. Peak demand is NOT reduced under a strict band (both controllers peak identically): the comfort constraint forces compressors on at peak.
5. Savings are larger in shoulder/mild months than in peak July, indicating coordination helps most where cooling demand is substantial but non-saturating.
6. The 20-zone extended case has high seed variance and is reported as approximate.
